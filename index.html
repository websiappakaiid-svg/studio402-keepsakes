No app download is required. Your keepsake is a high-performance private web application. When your partner opens your custom link or scans your QR code on iPhone or Android, it loads instantly in Safari, Chrome, or any mobile browser with full sound and animations.

Yes. You can search any track live in our editor using Apple Music / iTunes to stream a real high-fidelity song preview, or you can upload your own personal MP3, voice recording, or acoustic cover directly from your device.

How do I present this as a physical gift?

Upon claiming your keepsake, you receive both your private URL (`studio402.com/v/your-names`) and a high-resolution printable QR code card. You can slip the QR card inside an anniversary envelope, frame it, or attach it to a bouquet of flowers for an unforgettable physical reveal.
